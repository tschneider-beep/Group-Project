Our heirloom tomatoes are grown using traditional organic methods with no synthetic pesticides or fertilizers. Each variety has its own unique flavor profile — from the rich, smoky Cherokee Purple to the bright, fruity Yellow Pear. These are the tomatoes your grandparents remember.

Peak season runs from July through September. These tomatoes are only available during the warm summer months when conditions are just right. Once the first frost hits, the season ends — so stock up while you can!

  • Available: July – September
  • Peak flavor: August
  • Limited quantities each week

For best flavor, store tomatoes at room temperature away from direct sunlight. Do not refrigerate — cold temperatures destroy the flavor compounds that make heirlooms special.

  • Store at room temperature (65–75°F)
  • Keep out of direct sunlight
  • Best used within 3–5 days
  • Do not refrigerate
